SHADOWSTRIKE WEBv4

v4 — SUNSET UPDATE: warmer sunset lighting, brighter saturated world, bigger trees and clearer buildings. If you do not see v4 here, hard-refresh / open in a private tab — you are on a cached old version.

Original 50-player battle-royale-style match: you vs 49 bots on a big open map. Bots fight each other too, so the alive count falls even if you hide. Last one standing wins.

Original game — not Free Fire, COD, or any commercial game. ShadowStrike Web is made from scratch with original art, names and map. It is not made by or affiliated with Garena, Activision, or any publisher. Graphics are browser WebGL2 with PBR materials, soft shadows and ACES tone mapping — v4 adds a warm sunset look, saturated textures and bigger trees that are visible on Balanced too (bloom-style glow stays High / Ultra only, for phones). No proprietary upscaling or ray tracing is used, and no WebGPU renderer is bundled in this build (see README for why), and no real multiplayer is included in this version: the other 49 players are bots, because real human multiplayer needs a live server, which static web hosting cannot provide.

Map zones

Downtown · Old Port · Factory Row · Hill Town · Farm Fields · Quarry — loot is spread across all of them. Stay inside the shrinking zone ring.

How to play

  • Desktop: WASD move, mouse aim, click / hold shoot, Space jump, Shift sprint, C crouch, R reload, Q weapon switch, F medkit, right-mouse hold to aim / scope.
  • Mobile: Left joystick move, drag right side to aim, FIRE (large), JUMP, CROUCH, RELOAD, WEAPON, AIM and MEDKIT buttons on the right.
  • Pick up health, ammo, medkits and better weapons. Placement is shown when you are eliminated — #1 is a win.

Tip: on a phone, play in landscape — turn your phone sideways, tap Full screen, then Drop In. Balanced quality is recommended on phones. Ultra is desktop-only and may be slow on phones.
